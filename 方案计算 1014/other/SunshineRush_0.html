How We Mine the Data: Our Fair Value Assessment Protocol

Surveying the Claim (屋顶储量评估)

We use high-resolution aerial imagery and 3D modeling to determine your roof's "Proven Reserves" – the precise area suitable for high-efficiency mining.

Assessing the Ore Grade (阳光品位评级)

Leveraging EU PVGIS satellite data, we assign a grade (from A to AAA) to your location's sunlight, determining the richness of your solar resource.

Calculating the Asset Value (资产价值核算)

We apply a standardized financial model (NPV, IRR) to calculate your rooftop's 25-year lifecycle value, treating it as a world-class infrastructure asset.
